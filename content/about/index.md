**Vegan Theory Club** is a collective reading project focused on vegan theory, animal ethics, and liberation politics.

We read a wide range of texts such as academic philosophy, political theory, zines, manifestos, and fiction with a focus on how veganism intersects with race, gender, class, and ecology.

## How it works

Each week a new reading is posted to this site. There's no registration or membership; just read along and engage in whatever way works for you.

## Get involved

Want to suggest a reading or contribute? Open an issue or pull request on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/hamid-veganhomecooks/vegantheoryclub).
