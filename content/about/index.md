**Vegan Theory Club** is a collective reading project focused on vegan theory, animal ethics, and liberation politics.

We read a wide range of texts such as academic philosophy, political theory, zines, manifestos, and fiction with a focus on how veganism intersects with race, gender, class, and ecology.

## How it works

Each week a new reading is posted to this site. There's no registration or membership; just read along and engage in whatever way works for you. You can join us on the [Discord Server](https://discord.gg/pg9nVqgP4u) if you want to hang out. 

## Calendar 

📅 [Subscribe to the reading calendar](/readings.ics)

You can add all upcoming readings to your calendar app automatically. 
Most calendar apps support subscribing to an ICS feed — this means 
new readings will appear in your calendar as we add them, without 
you having to do anything.

**Google Calendar** — open [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com), 
click the **+** next to "Other calendars" → "From URL" → paste 
`https://vegantheoryclub.org/readings.ics`

**Apple Calendar** — File → New Calendar Subscription → paste 
`https://vegantheoryclub.org/readings.ics`

**Outlook** — Add calendar → Subscribe from web → paste 
`https://vegantheoryclub.org/readings.ics`

**Any other app** — look for "subscribe", "add by URL", or "ICS feed" 
and paste `https://vegantheoryclub.org/readings.ics`

Prefer to just import the dates once? Click the link above to download 
the file and open it — your calendar app will add the current readings 
as a one-time import.
